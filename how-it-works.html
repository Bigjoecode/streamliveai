How it works

From sign-up to output

Four steps, most of them under a minute. Here's the whole path a job takes.

1

Create your account

Sign up for StreamLive AI in just a few moments. Enter your details, create your account and get instant access to the platform.

2

Buy a credit pack (optional, any time)

Pick a pack, pay by card, bank transfer or crypto, and your balance updates as soon as the payment confirms.

3

Pick a tool and upload your input

Face swap, voice clone, image-to-video, or image edit — each has its own simple form for the photo, audio, or text it needs.

4

The job runs and credits are debited

One-shot tools debit a flat cost up front; livestream sessions are metered per second and debited when you end the session.

That's the whole loop

No hidden steps, no subscription trap — just credits in, output out, with a record of both.
