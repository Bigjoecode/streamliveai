Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what personal data StreamLive AI collects, why, and what rights you have over it. Because our Services involve faces and voices, we've tried to be direct about exactly what happens to that specific kind of data, rather than burying it in generic language.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text), or your Google/Facebook profile information if you sign up that way.
  • Face and voice data: photos, voice samples, and recordings you upload to use a specific tool. These are processed to generate your output and, for Voice Cloning specifically, a voice model is created with our AI provider (ElevenLabs) to fulfill your request.
  • Payment data: we never see or store your card number or wallet keys. Flutterwave (cards) and NOWPayments (crypto) handle payment details directly and only pass us confirmation that a payment succeeded.
  • Usage data: which tools you use, job history, and credit transactions, so your dashboard and billing history work correctly.

3. Why we collect it

To provide the Services you request, to bill you correctly, to detect and act on violations of our Terms of Service (including reviewing flagged content), to communicate with you about your account (password resets, job completion, purchase confirmations), and to improve the platform.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only where necessary to run the Services:

  • ElevenLabs — processes voice samples and text for Voice Cloning, Text to Speech, Voice Isolator, and Voice Changer.
  • Decart — processes reference face images and live video for Livestream (this runs as a direct connection between your browser and Decart, not routed through our server).
  • Flutterwave — processes card payments for credit purchases.
  • NOWPayments — processes cryptocurrency payments for credit purchases.
  • Google / Facebook — if you use social sign-in, they confirm your identity and share your basic profile information with us.

We do not sell your personal data, your face, or your voice to anyone, for any purpose.

5. Data retention

We retain account and job history data for as long as your account is active, so your dashboard, billing history, and job history continue to work. Uploaded voice samples are stored privately and are not shared beyond the AI provider used to process them. You can request deletion of your account and associated data (see Section 7).

6. Content moderation

Jobs that use a real face or voice can be flagged for review by our team. Reviewing a flag may involve a human looking at the job's output and metadata to assess whether it violates our Terms of Service. This is limited to jobs that are flagged, not a review of everything generated on the platform.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at [email protected]. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe and in line with applicable data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

8. Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and to remember referral links you've followed. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to [email protected].
